Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
Developer: Vinay Kumar
Contact: vinay.sirohi.developer@gmail.com

This policy covers these games on Google Play (each called "the game" below):

  • Color Escape: Block Puzzle
  • Ball Sort: Color Puzzle
  • Pipe Connect: Dot Puzzle
  • Water Puzzle: Grow the Garden
  • Vehicle Builder: Part Puzzle
  • Robot Run: Neon City Runner

In short: your game progress is stored on your device (and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, in your own Google account). To keep the games free, they show ads from Google AdMob; where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before ads use your personal data. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not sell your personal data. The games are for players aged 13 and older.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves on your device: level results (stars, best moves), coins, hints, items you unlocked, purchases you made (purchase reference numbers, so items are never granted twice), game-specific progress (such as the island, garden, garage or robots), settings (sound, music, vibration), your privacy choices and an age group (under 13, 13–15, or 16 and over). We do not store your birth year or date. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or using Settings → Delete my data removes it.

2. Age question

On first launch we ask for your year of birth to work out your age group, and we keep only the age group, never the year. Our games are made for players aged 13 and older: if the answer shows a player is under 13, the game can't be played, and nothing is collected, synced or shown as ads. Players aged 13–15 only see non-personalised ads.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

For players aged 13 and over the game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional reward ads that you choose to watch (for example for a hint or extra coins) and, occasionally, an ad between levels or runs (not if you bought "Remove ads"). Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads, to personalise them if you consent, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we use Google's consent platform to ask for your consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Players aged 13–15: ads are always non-personalised, and ad requests are marked as coming from a user under the age of consent.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive a purchase confirmation (the product and a purchase reference) but never your payment details. Purchases can be restored with Shop → Restore purchases.

5. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your game progress (levels and stars, coins, unlocked items, the game's own settings and your age group) in your Google Play Games account, so you can continue on another phone or after reinstalling. The copy is stored by Google in your own account; we cannot see it. Settings → Delete my data also deletes this copy, and you can remove a game's saved data in Google Play Games. If you are not signed in, your progress stays only on your device.

6. Online leaderboard (Robot Run only)

Robot Run has an online leaderboard run by Google Play Games. If you are 13 or over and signed in to Google Play Games, your best score is sent to Google together with your Play Games player ID, and your Play Games profile name and best score can be seen by other players. Google stores this data under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not receive your email address or contact details. You can manage or hide your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app. The other games have no leaderboard.

7. Analytics and error reports

The games do not currently use analytics or crash-reporting services. Information about errors is only kept in a small log on your device. If we add an analytics service in the future, we will update this policy first and show you the privacy notice again in the game.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is only processed by Google as described above (AdMob, Google Play and Google Play Games).

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because game data is stored on your device and in your own Google account, you can delete it with Settings → Delete my data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com. For any request, email vinay.sirohi.developer@gmail.com.

10. Children

Our games are made for players aged 13 and older and are not directed at children. If the age question shows that a player is under 13, the game shows a "made for players aged 13 and older" screen instead: it can't be played, no ads are requested, and no cloud save or leaderboard data is collected. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at vinay.sirohi.developer@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. A cloud save copy stays in your Google Play Games account until you delete it. We do not run our own servers and do not keep copies of your data.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update the date above and the game will show the privacy notice again the next time you open it.
